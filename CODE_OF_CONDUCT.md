# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Critique the work, not the person. Welcome people with different experience levels and backgrounds.

Harassment, discriminatory language, doxxing, threats, and sharing private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or block accounts to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to [the maintainer](https://github.com/pulkitxm). Do not post private details in an issue. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and disclose only what is needed to investigate and respond.
